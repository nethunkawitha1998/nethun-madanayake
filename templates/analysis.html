Data Collection & Analysis

Upload the exported questionnaire responses (CSV) to generate the full statistical analysis: construct reliability, correlations, hypothesis testing, and tailored MITRE ATT&CK-aligned recommendations.

Expected column format

The CSV must use the short survey codes defined in data_dictionary.csv (included in this repository), for example:

  • A5_JobRole, A6_YearsInCybersecurity, A8_IndustrySector, A9_OrganizationSize
  • B1…B10 — AI Tool Adoption
  • C1…C10 — AI Adoption Maturity
  • D1_1…D1_5 — Mean Time to Detect
  • D2_1…D2_5 — Mean Time to Respond
  • E1…E10 — Alert Triage Accuracy
  • F1…F10 — Analyst Productivity
  • G1_1…G1_8 — Trust in AI
  • G2_1…G2_8 — Explainability
  • H1…H10 — Organisational Factors

A raw Google Forms export uses full question text as column headers — rename these to the codes above before uploading. Missing scales are skipped automatically rather than causing an error.
